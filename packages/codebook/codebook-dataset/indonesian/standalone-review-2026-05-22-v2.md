# Indonesian Standalone Review - 2026-05-22 v2

This second review batch continues reducing Indonesian URL codebook reliance on
fused generated compounds.

Scope:

- Added another pool of short standalone Indonesian words.
- Preferred familiar, neutral public-address words from food, home, tools,
  places, craft, transport, plants, animals, materials, and everyday objects.
- Kept ASCII title-case forms and the twelve-letter URL readability limit.
- Avoided person labels, brands, adult terms, gambling, drugs, weapons,
  medicine, politics, religion, insults, debt, illness, and other sensitive
  domains from the shared codebook guide.
