# Indonesian Standalone Review - 2026-05-24

This third review batch reduces Indonesian URL codebook reliance on broad
generated noun/adjective and noun/object compounds.

Scope:

- Added more reviewed standalone Indonesian words before generated fallback
  compounds.
- Preferred familiar concrete words from food, home, tools, geography, plants,
  animals, craft, materials, transport, and public places.
- Kept ASCII title-case forms and the twelve-letter URL readability limit.
- Avoided person labels, brands, adult terms, gambling, drugs, weapons,
  medicine, politics, religion, insults, debt, illness, and other sensitive
  domains from the shared codebook guide.
- Kept obvious proper place names out of the shared codebook.
