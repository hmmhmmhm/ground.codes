# Indonesian Standalone Review - 2026-05-22

This review expands the Indonesian distributed codebook away from fused
template compounds.

Scope:

- Added a larger reviewed pool of short standalone Indonesian words.
- Used ASCII title-case forms so public URLs stay readable and stable.
- Preferred familiar neutral nouns from nature, home, food, tools, clothing,
  materials, buildings, and everyday objects.
- Avoided person names, brands, adult terms, gambling, drugs, weapons,
  medicine, politics, religion, insults, debt, illness, and other sensitive
  domains from the authoring guide.
- Kept every reviewed word at twelve letters or fewer.
